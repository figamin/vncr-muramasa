## botsu

Immersion tracking is based on [botsu](https://github.com/xoltia/botsu) by xoltia. The activity and goal models and the duration parsing in `utils/immersion_models.py` are ported from its Go code, and `utils/data_transfer.py` reads botsu's export format. botsu is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, the same license as this project; the text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
